Our story

Home, road, tree, and sun

A boutique Southern African travel house rooted in McGregor, Western Cape — looking outward across the subcontinent.

Who we are

Kimba African Journeys

We curate personalised experiences for travellers who prefer places only the locals know about. Our work spans guided tours for small groups and self-drive journeys for those with a more adventurous spirit — throughout Southern Africa, in both English and German.

Whether you travel as a single, a couple, or a group, we specialise in personal attention: the right lodge, the right pace, and the quiet corners that turn a holiday into something lasting.

Our base is in McGregor, a village in the Western Cape — not Cape Town itself, but close enough to the Cape’s beauty and far enough to remember why people come to Africa in the first place.
